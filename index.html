WHO WE ARE

About Graceland Foundation

Graceland Foundation is a Christian-based organisation committed to transforming communities through education, agriculture, family empowerment and support for vulnerable groups.

Our mission is to fight early marriages, support the elderly, empower young girls to excel in school and spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
