WHO WE ARE

About Graceland Foundation

Graceland Foundation is a Christian-based organisation dedicated to transforming communities by addressing the challenges faced by vulnerable groups, especially young girls, families and persons with disabilities.

We demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ through compassion, support, empowerment and community development initiatives that create hope and lasting change.

Our work focuses on education support, protection of girls, advocacy for the sanctity of life, assistance to vulnerable people and empowering families to become self-reliant.

Our Mission

To demonstrate the love of Christ by providing essential basic needs to school-going girls and persons with disabilities, advocating for the sanctity of life, and spreading the Gospel to maintain and cultivate hopeful, resilient communities.

Our Vision

A society transformed where every young girl and person with disability is valued, equipped and empowered to reach their full, God-given potential, rooted in the love and grace of Jesus Christ.

OUR VALUES

What We Stand For

Our work is guided by Christian values that shape how we serve communities.

Sanctity Of Life

We value and protect every life, promoting dignity, hope and opportunities for vulnerable people.

Compassion

We serve communities with love, kindness and a commitment to helping those in need.

Respect & Dignity

We believe every person deserves respect, fairness and equal opportunities.

Integrity & Unity

We work together with honesty, transparency and shared responsibility.

OUR PURPOSE

Why Graceland Foundation Exists

Many communities face challenges including early marriages, limited education opportunities, poverty and lack of support for vulnerable groups.

Graceland Foundation exists to respond with practical solutions that restore hope, strengthen families and create opportunities for a better future.

Through partnerships and community involvement, we believe lasting transformation is possible.
